# Escalation Matrix (synthetic, for demonstration)

## Level 1: desk head
A WARNING on any desk metric is reported to the desk head by end of day. No formal action is required unless the warning persists for three days.

## Level 2: Market Risk manager
A BREACH on any desk metric is escalated to the Market Risk manager on the same day. The manager reviews the attribution and decides whether the breach is a market move or a data issue.

## Level 3: Head of Market Risk
A breach that persists for two or more days, a stress breach, or any proposed limit change goes to the Head of Market Risk. The Head decides whether to approve a temporary excess.

## Human review queue for agent cases
An agent case goes to the human review queue when any of these hold: the attribution is ambiguous (the top two drivers are within 20% of each other), the confidence is low, a data anomaly is detected, or the case involves a limit calibration question. A risk manager must resolve the case before anything is sent onward.

## Data incidents
A failed jump check or a stale price is raised to the data owner within one hour. The breach is marked as provisional until the price is corrected and the metric is recalculated.

## Documentation
Every escalation records the time, the person notified, the evidence used and the decision. Records are kept for at least seven years.
